🔥 Knight's Isolation ❄️

Two knights. One shrinking board. Last knight who can move wins.

Each knight moves in an L like a chess knight (2 squares one way, then 1 to the side). Every square a knight leaves is gone for good — nobody can land there again, and a knight can't pass over a gone square on the 2-square part of its L. If it's your turn and your knight has nowhere legal to go, you lose.
